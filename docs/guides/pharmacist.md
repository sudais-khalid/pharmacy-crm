# Pharmacist guide

You look after medicines, stock and expiry, bring in supplier bills, order from suppliers, and handle what the counter cannot: returns, cancellations and credit beyond a limit. You can also sell at the counter. The [cashier guide](cashier.md) covers selling.

## Every morning

Your dashboard shows what needs doing:

- batches expiring soon;
- batches already expired that are still in stock;
- low stock;
- stock entries waiting for review;
- supply orders that are due.

The bell at the top lists the same alerts. Each alert links to the fix.

## Bringing in stock (Stock entry)

All four ways end in the same review screen. Nothing changes your stock until you post it.

- **Photo of a supplier bill:** take one or more photos of the bill, flat and in good light. The lines are read for you; check each one against the paper.
- **Photo of a stock list:** a handwritten or printed list, read the same way.
- **Type it in:** use the grid. **F2** jumps to the medicine, **Enter** moves to the next cell, **Ctrl+D** copies the cell above, and **Ctrl+Enter** posts.
- **Import a file:** an Excel or CSV file, such as opening stock or a supplier's price list.

When reviewing:

- A line is coloured until it is matched to a medicine. Pick the right one, or create a new medicine from the line.
- Check the batch, expiry, quantity and price on every line.
- The app remembers how each supplier names its products, so the next bill from them matches better.

## Expiry

- **Inventory > Expiry** lists what expires soon.
- Write a batch off (expired or damaged), or **set it aside** for return to the supplier so it is no longer sold.
- **Inventory > Supplier returns** prints the list for each supplier. Record the return there when the goods go back.
- To trace a batch for a recall, open the medicine and choose the batch: you see who bought it, with phone numbers.

## Ordering

- **Supply orders > Reorder** suggests what to order. It looks at stock, sales over the last 30 days, the supplier's delivery time and what is already on order.
- One click makes a draft order for each supplier. Approve it, then print it or send it by WhatsApp.
- When the goods arrive, receive them against the order. Anything that did not come stays pending.

## At the counter

- **Returns:** scan the QR code on the receipt, choose the lines and quantities, and say whether each goes back on the shelf or is written off as damaged. Then refund in cash, by card or as credit.
- **Cancel an invoice:** **Invoices**, open the bill, **Cancel**, and give a reason.

## Reports

Reports show sales, gross profit, purchases and stock, by day or month, and export to Excel. Expenses and net profit are for the Pharmacy Admin.
