# Cashier guide

You run the counter: sales, receipts and customers' accounts. You do not see cost prices or profit.

## Signing in

Open the sign-in address your pharmacy gave you (it ends in `/p/<pharmacy code>/login`), then enter your username and password. The counter opens straight away.

If your pharmacy uses the cash drawer, open it first: count the cash in the drawer and enter it as the opening float.

## Making a sale

1. Press **F2** and scan the barcode, or type part of the name. Pick the medicine with the arrow keys and **Enter**.
2. Change the quantity with **+** and **-**. Remove a line with **Delete**.
3. Choose how the customer pays: **F5** cash, **F6** card, **F7** credit (udhaar, for a saved customer only).
4. For cash, press **F8** and type what the customer handed over. The change is shown.
5. Press **F9** to complete the sale. The receipt prints.

The counter always sells the batch that expires first, and never sells an expired one.

For a walk-in customer you can type a name and phone number on the bill. Use **Save as customer** if they will come back or buy on credit.

## Customers who buy on credit

- A credit sale needs a saved customer, and it stops at the customer's credit limit. A pharmacist can allow more.
- When a customer pays off their balance, open **Customers**, choose them, then **Receive payment**.

## Receipts

- **Ctrl+P** reprints the last receipt.
- **Invoices** lists the bills you made, so you can reprint any of them.

## Returns

Returns are made by a pharmacist. Send the customer to them with the receipt (they scan its QR code).

## Closing your drawer

At the end of your shift, open **Cash drawer** and count the cash. The app tells you what should be there. If the count is different, add a note saying why.

## Keys

Press **?** on any screen for its keys. Your pharmacy can print a shortcut card for the counter from that list.
