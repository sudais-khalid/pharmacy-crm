# Pharmacy Admin guide

You run the pharmacy on the app: staff, roles, branding, prices, expenses, reports and settings. You can do everything a pharmacist or cashier can; see the [pharmacist](pharmacist.md) and [cashier](cashier.md) guides.

## First day

The dashboard offers **Set up your pharmacy** until you finish it. It has six steps:

1. **Profile and logo.** Add the address, phone and logo that print on every receipt.
2. **Receipt format.** Choose the size your printer uses: 80 mm, 58 mm or A4.
3. **Staff.** Give each person their own account. Cashiers see no cost prices or profit.
4. **Opening stock.** Enter what is on the shelves today, as a photo of the stock register, an Excel file, or typed in.
5. **Customers who owe you.** Enter what each one owes today, so their accounts start right.
6. **What you owe suppliers.** The same for each distributor.

Only you can enter balances from before the app.

## Staff and roles

- **Users** adds staff and issues sign-in links or temporary passwords (send them by WhatsApp). You can reset a password, sign someone out everywhere, or switch an account off.
- **Roles** lets you make your own roles, for example a senior cashier who may also cancel invoices. Every change is recorded in **Activity**.

## Money

- **Expenses** records rent, salaries, bills and other running costs, with a photo of the receipt if you have one. Paying a supplier is not an expense.
- Cash taken from a drawer for an expense lowers what that drawer should hold.
- A mistake is cancelled with a reason; it is never deleted.
- **Cash drawer** shows every session and its difference. Large differences notify you.

## Reports and the dashboard

- The dashboard shows:
  - today and the month so far;
  - the last 30 days against the 30 before;
  - profit by category;
  - expenses, drawer differences;
  - what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers.
- **Reports** has sales by day or month, with the period before for comparison, and by cashier, payment method and category.
- **Profit and loss** takes sales after discounts and returns, then subtracts the cost of the batches sold, stock written off and expenses.
- Every report prints on your letterhead and exports to Excel.

## Your data

**Settings > Your data** downloads every record of the pharmacy as a ZIP, with an Excel summary. Passwords are never included. The download is recorded.

## Subscription

When the subscription is about to end you get a notice a week before. After it ends, staff can keep working for a few days with a banner, then sign-in stops until it is renewed. Your records are kept either way. Contact your software provider to renew.
